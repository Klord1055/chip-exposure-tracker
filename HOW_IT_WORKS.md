## How it works

**Exposure.** China revenue = annual revenue x China share of revenue. Companies are tiered by share: Low (under 10%), Moderate (10 to 25%), High (25 to 40%), Severe (over 40%). Shares come from the period named in the table, so they mix annual and quarterly disclosures; edit them with the latest filing.

**Scenarios.** Each scenario sets the share of China revenue lost for chip designers and for equipment makers, plus how much of that lost revenue is resold to other regions by year 3. Every slider can be changed.

**Profit and value.** Operating profit lost = revenue lost x incremental margin (lost sales carry higher margins than average because fixed costs stay). Value at risk = profit lost x (1 minus a 15% tax rate) x the earnings multiple.

**Recovery path.** Net revenue lost in year t = revenue lost x (1 minus recapture x t / 3), so redirected sales ramp up over three years.

**Monte Carlo.** For each company, the loss rate is drawn from a beta distribution centered on the scenario value, and the China share moves by a normal shock with the relative standard deviation you set. The app adds the basket's revenue lost in each simulation and reports the mean, median, 5th and 95th percentiles.

**Limits.** This is a scenario tool, not a forecast. Starting figures come from company filings and earnings releases but periods differ and numbers change each quarter. Not investment advice.
